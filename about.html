About Numora Tuition

Personal, exam-focused tuition from someone who has recently been through the A-Level Maths and Further Maths journey.

Hi, I'm David.

I founded Numora Tuition after recently completing A-Level Maths and Further Maths myself. Having experienced these courses first-hand, I understand the challenges students face and the techniques that genuinely improve exam performance.

My goal is to help students build confidence, strengthen their understanding and develop the exam technique needed to achieve their target grades.

Every lesson is personalised, ensuring that students receive support tailored to their own strengths, weaknesses and goals.
